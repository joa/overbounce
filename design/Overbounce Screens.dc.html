SCREENSDecided menu screens — for implementation

The four non-HUD screens, as decided. Shell is shared with Overbounce Settings: 224px left rail on #0e0e12, 60px header, #15151b cards on #101014, 1px #22222c seams, 22/28/18 body padding, 14px gaps, footer commit bar. Type is Barlow Condensed for headings and numerals, JetBrains Mono for data. Dim text is #8a8a96; #4a4a54 means unavailable and nothing else. Physics and camera are declared per course and chosen at start — see Settings. The HUD lives in Overbounce HUD spec, the post-run screens in Overbounce Results.
