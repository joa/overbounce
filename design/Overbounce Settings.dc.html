SSettings

Deliberately small. The repo has 38 URL parameters and almost all of them are diagnostics — they stay in the URL. What surfaces here is only what a player has a reason to change: the two that alter how the game plays (physics mode and the frame-rate cap, which really does change jump height), the two that alter what the HUD tells you (OB help, debug panel), and one switch for how it looks — faithful 1999 or modern, exactly the pairing docs/url-parameters.md already documents. Advanced sits behind a disclosure and prints the URL it would produce, so a setting and a bug report are the same string.

CHANGES PHYSICS
The simulation steps at a fixed tick, independent of what the browser paints — the canvas is vsync-limited and sits at 60fps whatever this says. Velocity is snapped to whole units every step, so the tick decides how much gravity is rounded away: 125 is the competitive standard because it jumps highest.

Modern
AgX tone mapping, SSAO, FXAA, a real shadow map steered by the light grid, lava bloom and heat shimmer. All of it deliberately gentle — an effect that blooms into a doorway costs you information you navigate by.
Faithful 1999
What Quake actually drew: no tone curve, no ambient occlusion, no aberration, and Quake's own blob shadow under the player. Lightmaps and multipass shaders as shipped.
