SHUD spec — locked from 2a

One layout, six runtime states — RUNNING, IDLE, FREERUN, FINISHED, DEAD and PAUSED. Anchors are fixed: top-left is the clock and only the clock (timer, PB, ghost delta, splits, 236px column, right-aligned numerals), top-right is identity plus optional debug, bottom-centre is the speed instrument, bottom-left is the overbounce readout, bottom-right is vitals. Nothing is ever drawn in the middle 60% except a modal. Colours are the repo's: speed ramp from speedColor(), armour dimmed to #4a4a54 at zero, OB letters from overbounce.ts. Every state below is the same DOM with elements switched on and off — the same classList.toggle('hidden') discipline hud.ts already uses. Read the six state frames first (Sa Sb Sc Sd Se Sh), then the two reference cards — Sg the overbounce readout and Sf anchors and tokens.
